Hunspell dictionary after letters followed by a nukta
were replaced by a sed script with the corresponding precomposed glyph. 
The Hunspell dictionary contains a total of 15990 entries and
344 possible compounds were found, the last one having
to be discarded; 343 compounds are thus left.
The third column gives the
total number of occurrences in the dictionary.
