The following are statistics of occurrences of compounds in the 
aspell6-hi-0.02-0 dictionary after letters followed by a nukta
were replaced by a sed script with the corresponding precomposed glyph. 
That dictionary contains a total of 83514 entries. A total
of 607 possible compounds are found and the first entries where
those compounds occur is displayed. The fourth column gives the
total number of occurrences; 195 compounds would be required by
only one occurrence in the dictionary and
only 356 occur in more than two entries. Note that it
is possible that the same precomposed glyphs occurs twice in the
same entry; the script that generated the following table does not check.
